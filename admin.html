    Add to the banner

    Change any wording on the website: headings, menu, descriptions, group names, buttons and your contact details. Type over the text, then press Publish changes. “Reset” puts the original wording back. Words in curly brackets, like {shipsTo}, are filled in automatically, so leave them as they are.
